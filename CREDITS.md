## Adhan notification audio

The bundled Android notification Adhan is downloaded during GitHub Actions build from Wikimedia Commons file `Adhan.ogg`, published by its author under CC0 1.0.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adhan.ogg

The workflow converts it to `res/raw/adhan.wav` for Android notification playback.
